\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Microcontrollers: TinyML}
\begin{itemize}
\item Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense: a 64 MHz Arm Cortex-M4F chip (Nordic nRF52840) with 1 MB of flash (storage) and 256 KB of RAM.
\item It has a microphone and a motion sensor (IMU, inertial measurement unit).
\item What fits: keyword spotting, motion and vibration classification, small vision models. Example: an always-on vibration classifier watching a machine.
\item What does not: language models. A different tool for a different job.
\end{itemize}
{\tiny (Ref: Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense documentation; Nordic nRF52840 product page)}
