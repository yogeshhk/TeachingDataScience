\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Why Authors Are Wary of AI}
\begin{itemize}
\item Your name goes on the cover. Your voice and your judgment are the product.
\item A tool that writes fluent text in seconds feels like a threat to both.
\item Publishers and readers care about originality, accuracy and trust.
\item So the question is not whether AI can write your book. It is where AI can help without replacing the author.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{The Rule of This Seminar}
\begin{center}
\Large
\textbf{You write.}

\medskip
\textbf{AI assists around the writing.}
\end{center}

\medskip
\begin{itemize}
\item The text of your book, article or paper is written by you, by hand.
\item AI helps with the work that surrounds the text: ideas, research, diagrams, review, layout checks, emails.
\item Every example today uses the CRAFT framework, so you can reuse the pattern on your own tasks.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
