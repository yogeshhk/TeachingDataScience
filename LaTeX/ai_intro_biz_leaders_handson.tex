\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Mini Hands-On Activities}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Task 1:} Generate a meeting summary from a transcript in Teams.\\
    \textit{Prompt: ``Summarize this meeting. List decisions made, open questions, and action items with owners.''}
  \item \textbf{Task 2:} Create a product pitch deck using Copilot in PowerPoint.\\
    \textit{Prompt: ``Create a 5-slide investor pitch for [product]. Include problem, solution, market size, traction, and ask.''}
  \item \textbf{Task 3:} Use Copilot in Excel to extract trends from last quarter's data.\\
    \textit{Prompt: ``Which SKUs had the highest revenue growth quarter-on-quarter? Show me a chart.''}
  \item \textbf{Task 4:} Draft a strategic response to a customer escalation in Outlook.\\
    \textit{Prompt: ``Write an empathetic yet professional response to this customer complaint. Offer a resolution and a follow-up date.''}
\end{itemize}

\vspace{0.3em}
\textbf{Debrief:} What surprised you? What felt intuitive? Which task could save you 30 minutes tomorrow?

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Beyond Copilot, The Broader AI Tool Landscape}

Copilot is one tool. The ecosystem is much wider:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini:} General-purpose assistants for research, writing, analysis
  \item \textbf{Perplexity AI:} Real-time web research with citations, replaces preliminary analyst work
  \item \textbf{Jasper / Copy.ai:} Marketing content at scale, ad copy, SEO articles, campaign variations
  \item \textbf{Salesforce Einstein:} AI embedded in CRM, forecasting, next-best-action, auto-summaries
  \item \textbf{Notion AI:} Document intelligence, meeting notes, project summaries
  \item \textbf{Otter.ai:} Real-time meeting transcription and summarization
  \item \textbf{Custom internal AI apps:} RAG-based Q\&A on your own documents, policies, and knowledge base
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Key principle for leaders:} Pick tools that integrate with your existing workflows. Adoption fails when AI requires extra steps.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Linking Personal Gains to Organizational Value}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Translate individual productivity into team outcomes:
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Time saved per person $\times$ team size = hours of capacity reclaimed per week
      \item Clarity gained from AI-assisted analysis $\rightarrow$ faster, better decisions
      \item Automation of repetitive work $\rightarrow$ higher employee satisfaction and retention
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Example calculation:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item 10 leaders save 2 hrs/week using AI for meeting summaries and report drafting
      \item At an average fully-loaded cost of \textsf{\small INR 5,000/hr} = \textsf{\small INR 1,00,000/week} in reclaimed value
      \item Annual: \textsf{\small INR 50 lakhs}, from one AI tool, one habit change
    \end{itemize}
  \item Identify cultural enablers: psychological safety to experiment, top-down permission to use AI tools, sharing wins across teams
  \item \textbf{Output:} Personal AI Productivity Action Plan, one habit to start this week
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}
