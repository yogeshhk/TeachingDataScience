\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Program Objectives}
By the end of this program, leaders will be able to:
\begin{itemize}
\item Understand what AI, ML, GenAI, LLMs, and Agents are, and what they \textbf{cannot} do
\item Know where AI fits across the \textbf{SDLC}: requirements $\rightarrow$ dev $\rightarrow$ test $\rightarrow$ ops $\rightarrow$ support
\item Evaluate AI investments: \textbf{ROI, FinOps, pricing}, own LLM vs. integrate
\item Gain hands-on exposure to \textbf{building and using AI tools}
\item Establish \textbf{governance, security, and adoption} principles
\item Create real \textbf{internal use-cases} for their teams
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
