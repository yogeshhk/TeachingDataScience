\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Where Small Models Fall Short}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Tool calling}: \texttt{gemma-2-2b-it} never called the tool through the standard interface (0 of 5 tries), while Qwen3 1.7B did (5 of 5). It varies by model, so test yours.
\item \textbf{Numbers}: given two temperatures it said that 10.2 is higher than 11.4, five times out of five (an earlier run on stub data).
\item \textbf{Hidden thinking}: Qwen3 1.7B spent 126 of 128 tokens on reasoning that never reached the answer on open-ended prompts, so speed figures say little about answer length.
\item \textbf{Planning}: on a two-city comparison the text ReAct agent was wrong in 5 of 5 tries; the same task as a fixed workflow worked 5 of 5.
\item \textbf{Evidence is thin}: one machine, one day, small samples. Nothing here was evaluated in Hindi or Marathi.
\end{itemize}
{\tiny (Ref: our runs, 19 October 2026; model cards on Hugging Face)}
\end{frame}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Health and Safety}
\begin{itemize}
\item Health-related helpers are \textbf{decision support only}: no diagnosis, no dosing.
\item If the input suggests an emergency, the reply is to refer, using wording from official guidance and a clinical reviewer, not from the model.
\item Answer only from supplied, reviewed guidance, show the source, and say ``I do not know'' when it is not covered.
\item Measure Marathi and Hindi quality on an evaluation set before use; keep patient data on the device.
\item Check the legal position for clinical decision-support software before any pilot. Not yet checked here.
\end{itemize}
{\tiny (Ref: FrugalAI docs/safety.md)}
\end{frame}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Free Is Not Always Open}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Qwen3 1.7B}: Apache-2.0, a standard open-source licence.
\item \textbf{Gemma 2 2B}: tagged ``gemma'': you agree to Google's terms of use and a prohibited-use policy. Free to download and use, not an open-source licence.
\item \textbf{LM Studio}: free to use; the tool itself is not open source.
\item The awesome list's bar is ``free to use and open to all'' with downloadable weights and no per-token charge, and each entry names its licences.
\item Read the licence before you build a product on a model.
\end{itemize}
{\tiny (Ref: Hugging Face model cards for Qwen/Qwen3-1.7B and google/gemma-2-2b-it)}
\end{frame}
