\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Building Your 6--12 Month AI Roadmap}

Agree on these with your leadership team before leaving today:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{3--5 Priority Use Cases:} Ranked by ROI $\times$ feasibility. Start with quick wins.
  \item \textbf{Investment Areas:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Tooling: AI platform licenses, API access, data infrastructure
      \item People: 1--2 AI champions per function, AI governance lead
      \item Training: This workshop $\rightarrow$ team-level AI literacy programs
    \end{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Governance:} Finalize AI usage policy, approval workflow, audit mechanism, before deployment.
  \item \textbf{Milestones:}
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Month 1--2: AI policy published. Quick-win tools rolled out. Champions identified.
      \item Month 3--4: 2--3 AI use cases live in production with baseline metrics tracked.
      \item Month 5--6: AI ROI report to board. FinOps dashboards live. Governance review.
      \item Month 7--12: AI embedded in core business processes. Organizational AI maturity assessed.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{How to Drive AI Adoption, The Leadership Playbook}

\textbf{What kills AI adoption:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Random experimentation without shared learning, 20 teams, 20 tools, no coordination
  \item Fear and resistance, employees worried AI threatens their jobs
  \item No clear ownership, everyone waits, no one acts
\end{itemize}

\textbf{What works:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{AI Champions:} 1--2 enthusiastic adopters per team. Give them time, budget, and air cover.
  \item \textbf{Visible leadership use:} When the CEO and leadership team visibly use AI tools, adoption accelerates across the org.
  \item \textbf{Psychological safety:} Reward experimentation, not just success. Celebrate learning from failed AI pilots.
  \item \textbf{Shared learning:} Monthly AI wins newsletter / internal community. What worked, what didn't, how to replicate.
  \item \textbf{Quick wins first:} Show business impact within 30 days. Momentum is everything.
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{What AI Means for Your Workforce}

\textbf{AI creates new roles:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Prompt Engineers, AI Product Managers, AI Ethics Officers
  \item Data Curators, AI Operations Managers, Evaluation Specialists
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Existing roles transforming:}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Marketing Managers $\rightarrow$ AI-augmented content strategists (directing AI, not creating manually)
  \item Finance Analysts $\rightarrow$ AI-augmented insight generators (interpreting AI outputs, not building models)
  \item HR Managers $\rightarrow$ AI-augmented people strategists (using AI for workforce analytics)
  \item Customer Success $\rightarrow$ AI-augmented relationship managers (AI handles Tier-1, humans handle Tier-3)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Skills to develop across your org:} AI literacy, critical evaluation of AI outputs, prompt communication skills, data interpretation, and ethical judgment.

\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{The Future of AI in Business, Where This Is Going}

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Agentic AI at scale (2025--2026):} AI agents handling end-to-end business processes, from customer onboarding to invoice processing, with minimal human intervention
  \item \textbf{Multimodal AI:} AI that simultaneously processes text, images, voice, and video, richer customer insights, richer products
  \item \textbf{Physical AI:} Robotics powered by AI entering manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, not just software
  \item \textbf{AI-native organizations:} Companies built from day one around AI capabilities, disrupting incumbents who bolt AI onto legacy processes
  \item \textbf{Personalization at individual scale:} Every customer interaction, product, and service tailored to the individual, at zero marginal cost
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Leader takeaway:} Plan for AI-augmented operations in the near term. Invest in AI literacy, governance, and data foundations now. The organizations that build these capabilities today will be the category leaders of 2027.

\begin{frame}[fragile]\frametitle{Limits on AI, Staying Grounded}

Setting honest expectations prevents failed projects and board-level disappointment:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{No true reasoning:} AI finds patterns, not cause-and-effect. Human judgment remains essential.
  \item \textbf{Accuracy limits:} Every AI system has an error rate. Critical decisions need human review.
  \item \textbf{Data dependency:} AI is only as good as the data it is trained or grounded on. Data quality is a prerequisite, not an afterthought.
  \item \textbf{Black-box risk:} Most powerful AI models cannot explain their reasoning, regulatory and trust risk in high-stakes domains.
  \item \textbf{Maintenance burden:} AI systems drift over time as the world changes. Ongoing investment in monitoring and updates is required.
  \item \textbf{Change management:} Technology is the easy part. People, process, and culture change is the hard part.
\end{itemize}

\end{frame}
